Study guide

Study for the amateur radio license exams from the official NCVEC question pools: Technician 2026–2030, General 2023–2027 and Extra 2024–2028. The Guide is the answer to every question, colored by topic family, with the terms and formulas each question uses one tap away in the Glossary and Formulas. The Mastery map shows every question at a glance. Or browse by subelement and group, drill with flashcards, or take a practice exam drawn the way the real one is — one question from each group — and scored against the passing score.

Your answers are counted in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.

The question pools

NCVEC, public domain

Each pool is valid for a fixed window. The practice exam draws one question from each group, which is how the pool sets the exam’s subject mix.

Questions and figures are from the National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators (NCVEC) amateur question pools, which the NCVEC releases into the public domain.

Just Another Ham Study is not affiliated with the FCC, the NCVEC, the ARRL or HamStudy.org (an independent site, linked in the header), and is not an official exam-prep service. Check the current pool and the rules with your Volunteer Examiner Coordinator before you test.
